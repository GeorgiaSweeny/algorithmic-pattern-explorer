Instrument overview

17 items across six new types, on top of Study 1's own 8 retained/rebuilt computational-thinking concepts — see dissertation/Study2-Design-Plan.md §3-4 for why each type exists and what gap in Study 1 it targets. Spread across 9 of the app's 14 generators, including both hybrids (Perlin Sierpinski, Voronoi Islamic), so no single item type or generator carries the whole compositional-reasoning result.

TypeCountTestsScoring

spectrum 4 Placing a pattern on the app's own stochastic ↔ deterministic scale Binary, single correct index (5 bins)

order 1 Sequence of operations — put a generator's real stages in the order they actually run Exact-position-match (headline score) + positions-correct count (secondary)

Per-item-type breakdown

The direct fix for Study 1's diagnostic gap (design plan §5): reporting by item type, not only by concept, so a gain concentrated in one format but flat in another is visible rather than averaged away. It is: concept-match and spectrum gained, cause held flat, predict dropped, and the two new compositional formats (node-select, order) started and stayed near floor on exact match, with only partial credit showing any post-exploration movement.

node-select/order exact-match scores near zero at both phases aren't a floor-effect data problem — they're the expected shape for a strict full-set/full-sequence match with 3-6 correct elements per item (design plan §5's own rationale for scoring partial credit as a secondary measure alongside exact match). Partial credit is where the compositional items actually show a post-exploration signal; exact match mostly doesn't move at n=5.
